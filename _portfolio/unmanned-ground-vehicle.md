## Project Overview

Development of an integrated positioning and navigation system for an Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV), combining GNSS/RTK, LiDAR, IMU and wheel odometry for localisation and autonomous navigation.

## Objectives

- Develop a multi-sensor positioning and localisation system.
- Integrate GNSS/RTK positioning with LiDAR-based localisation.
- Investigate the accuracy and reliability of different positioning and navigation approaches.
- Enable real-time mapping, localisation and navigation.

## Technical Approach

- **GNSS/RTK:** High-precision absolute positioning and heading estimation.
- **LiDAR:** Environmental perception and scan-based localisation.
- **IMU:** Measurement of angular velocity and linear acceleration.
- **Wheel odometry:** Estimation of vehicle motion.
- **Sensor fusion:** Integration of GNSS, IMU and wheel odometry using ROS 2.
- **SLAM:** LiDAR-based mapping and localisation using SLAM algorithms.

## Key Contributions

- Integration of GNSS/RTK, LiDAR and IMU hardware.
- Configuration of ROS 2 sensor drivers and communication interfaces.
- Development of real-time positioning and data acquisition pipelines.
- Integration and evaluation of sensor fusion and localisation algorithms.
- Comparison of RTK positioning with LiDAR-based localisation results.

## Project Outcomes

Development of a multi-sensor UGV positioning and navigation platform, enabling investigation of high-precision localisation, sensor fusion and autonomous navigation in real-world environments.
